Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

NewsByFocus ("the app", also available at www.newsbyfocus.com) is provided by . Contact: .

1. No account, no tracking

You do not need to register or sign in. The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking tools. It does not access your advertising identifier, location, contacts, photos or any other device permission.

2. Data stored only on your device

Your language, the countries and topics you choose, your own topics and the optional name you enter are stored only in the app's local storage on your device, together with the last downloaded edition so the app can open without an internet connection. You can delete this data at any time by deleting the app (or clearing the website data in your browser).

3. What is sent to our servers

To show the news, the app downloads the editions from www.newsbyfocus.com (hosted by GitHub, Inc.). For your own topics, translations into your language and the preparation of a newly chosen country, the app sends the following to our server app.newsbyfocus.workers.dev (operated on Cloudflare, Inc.): the topic you entered (e.g. "inflation"), the country code and the language code. No name, device identifier or account is sent.

So that daily summaries of your topics can be prepared every morning, the server keeps the topic text, country, language and the date it was last requested. This entry is not linked to you or your device and is deleted automatically if nobody requests the topic for 14 days.

As with every internet connection, the hosting providers (GitHub, Cloudflare) process your IP address and technical connection data (time, browser/app type) briefly to deliver the content and protect the service. We do not combine this data with any other data and cannot identify you with it.

4. Other services the app loads content from

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts (Google Ireland Ltd.) and country flags from flagcdn.com. These providers receive your IP address when the content is loaded.

5. Artificial intelligence

News is selected, summarised and translated on our servers using the Google Gemini API. Only news texts from public news sources and the topic terms described above are processed; no personal data about you is sent to Google.

6. Links to publishers

When you open a story's source, the publisher's website opens. Its own privacy policy applies there.

7. Children

The app is a general news app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Your rights (GDPR, KVKK)

Because we do not store personal data that can be linked to you, there is generally nothing we could provide, correct or delete. You can nevertheless contact us at any time at with questions about data protection or to exercise your rights (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, data portability). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the current version is published on this page.

Die Nachrichten werden auf unseren Servern mit der Google-Gemini-API ausgewählt, zusammengefasst und übersetzt. Verarbeitet werden nur Texte öffentlicher Nachrichtenquellen und die oben beschriebenen Themenbegriffe; personenbezogene Daten über Sie werden nicht an Google gesendet.
